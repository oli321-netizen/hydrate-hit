Caffeine pouch vs energy drink

An energy drink is a can you finish. A caffeine pouch is a lip pouch you tuck. FluxHit is the second: 80 mg caffeine, 60 mg L-theanine, light electrolytes, no nicotine.

What is in a caffeine pouch

FluxHit is a nicotine-free caffeine pouch. One pouch is 80 mg caffeine — in the same ballpark as a small coffee or a typical energy-drink serving, without the cup, the sugar theatre, or a second liquid to carry. Alongside that: 60 mg L-theanine, 50 mg sodium, 50 mg potassium, B6 and B12. Full stack on the home stack.

Energy drinks vary wildly. Some are mostly sugar and a mystery “blend”. This pouch does not pretend to be a meal or a pint of water. It is an oral pouch: tuck, sit, get on with it.

When a pouch is the better tool

  • You want caffeine without another drink in your hand.
  • You want theanine in the same tuck, not a guess.
  • You do not want nicotine. This is not a nicotine pouch.
  • You like the tuck — see snus alternative if that is the ritual you mean.

When a drink is the better tool: you are thirsty. Drink water. The pouch is not a bottle. Light salts in milligrams live on light electrolyte pouches. The no-nicotine position is on nicotine-free pouches.
